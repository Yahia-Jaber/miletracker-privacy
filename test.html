MileTracker: help me test it

About 2 minutes. Android phones only.

MileTracker logs your drives by itself, so your mileage deduction has a record. It is in Google's testing stage, and I need drivers to run it and tell me what is wrong.

  1. Join the tester group. Sign in with the Google account that is on your phone, then tap "Join group". 1. JOIN GROUP
  2. Become a tester. Tap "Become a tester", then "Download it on Google Play". 2. BECOME A TESTER
  3. Drive like normal. At the end of each shift, open the app and look at your trips.

Keep it installed for three weeks. Pro is free while the app is in testing. If the Play page says you are not eligible, wait an hour and reload it.

Something wrong or confusing? Write appsupport77@gmail.com. One line is enough.
