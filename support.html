How trip tracking works

  • MileTracker notices when you start driving, records the trip from your phone's location, and ends it when you stop. The app does not need to be open.
  • Your trips stay on your phone. There are no accounts and no cloud copy.
  • Distances come from GPS and are estimates. Check a trip before you rely on it for taxes or reimbursement.

Trips not recording, or starting late

  • Android: in your phone's settings, set Location for MileTracker to "Allow all the time", allow Physical activity and Notifications, and turn off battery optimization for MileTracker.
  • iPhone: in Settings, MileTracker, set Location to "Always" with Precise Location on, and allow Motion & Fitness and Notifications.
  • Open the app once after reinstalling it, updating it or restarting your phone, so tracking can start again.
  • A trip can begin a little after you pull away, and GPS can drop in garages and tunnels. You can add or correct a trip by hand in the app.

MileTracker Pro

  • Pro unlocks exporting your trips as CSV and Excel files. Tracking, trip history and alerts stay free.
  • Cancel or manage on iPhone: Settings, your name, Subscriptions.
  • Cancel or manage on Android: Google Play, your profile picture, Payments and subscriptions, Subscriptions.
  • Restore Pro after reinstalling or on a new phone: open Account, then tap Restore Purchases. Use the same Apple or Google account you bought it with.
  • Refunds are handled by the store that billed you: Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com, Google Play through your order history.

Your data

Your trips are stored only on your phone. To remove them, delete trips in the app or uninstall it. Uninstalling removes all trip data, so export first if you need a copy. There is no server copy for us to delete.
