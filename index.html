Privacy Policy for MileTracker

Effective date: September 25, 2026

MileTracker is a mileage tracking app for Android that records trips from your phone's location and lets you export them. It is published on Google Play under the package name io.github.yahia_jaber.mileagetracker.

Summary

MileTracker keeps your trip data on your device. The app:

  • uses location data to detect, record and measure trips;
  • stores trip history, settings and app data locally on your phone;
  • has no accounts and no cloud sync;
  • does not sell your personal information;
  • does not show third party ads;
  • does not upload your trips or your location history to our servers.

Information the app accesses

  • Location, precise, in the foreground and in the background, to detect when a trip starts, to record the route and distance, and to detect when it ends. Background location is what makes automatic trip detection work while the app is not open.
  • Local storage, to save trip history, settings and the export files you create.
  • Notifications, to show the tracking status and trip alerts.

How your data is used

Your data is used only to provide the app's features: automatic trip detection, mileage calculation, trip history, alerts, and exports when you request them.

Data sharing

MileTracker does not sell, rent or share your trip history or location data with advertisers, data brokers or analytics providers. Your data leaves the app only when you export or share it yourself, or when you subscribe (see Subscriptions and payments below).

Exports and diagnostics you send

Your trips are stored on your device. If you export trips as a CSV or spreadsheet file, or send a diagnostics file, the file leaves your device only through the share sheet or a folder you pick, to the app, folder or person you choose. A diagnostics file can contain trip locations, timestamps, GPS quality data, app settings and device model information. We receive it only if you send it to us, and we use it only to investigate the problem you report.

Subscriptions and payments

MileTracker offers an optional Pro subscription that unlocks the export features. Trips, tracking and alerts stay free. Purchases are made through Google Play and are billed by Google under its own terms. We never see or store your card or bank details.

To recognise your subscription across devices and app installs, we use RevenueCat (RevenueCat, Inc.) as our subscription processor. RevenueCat receives a random identifier for your app install, your purchase tokens and subscription status from Google Play, your device type and your app version. It does not receive your name, your email address, your trips or your location. RevenueCat's privacy policy is at https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy.

You can cancel a subscription at any time from the Subscriptions page in Google Play. Cancelling stops future renewals; Pro stays active until the end of the period you have paid for. A free trial becomes a paid subscription when it ends unless you cancel before then. Purchase records are kept for as long as your subscription is active and afterwards only as long as accounting and tax rules require.

Data retention and deletion

Because trip data is stored on your device, retention is controlled by you. Your data stays on your phone until you delete trips in the app, clear the app's storage or uninstall the app. Uninstalling removes all trip data unless you exported it first. There is no server copy to delete.

Security

MileTracker reduces privacy exposure by keeping trip data on the device. No software or storage method can guarantee absolute security, so keep your phone protected with a screen lock.

Children's privacy

MileTracker is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from them.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated from time to time. Changes are published on this page with a new effective date.
